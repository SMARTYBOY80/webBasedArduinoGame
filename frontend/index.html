Flappy Bird (Arduino Game) Made by Jack Smart & Jamie MacDonald

Welcome to our clone of the popular retro game Flappy Bird. We created this for one of our core modules in our Computing Science Major, and it took us 3 weeks in development (including, debugging and testing). Controls:
Jump = The push button you've connected.
Reset Game = Hover your hand over the senor you've connected.
